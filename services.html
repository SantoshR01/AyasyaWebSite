Services

Software and product delivery, powered by AI.

Every service uses the same AI-native workflow, so you get a faster pace, more test coverage and better documentation, with experienced people accountable for the result.

01

AI-native product development

We design and build web applications, platforms and APIs from scratch. Our engineers work with coding, testing and review agents throughout, so routine work moves quickly and their time goes into architecture, edge cases and the details users notice.

Ideal for: new products, MVPs, major features, and rebuilds of ageing systems.

What you get

Technical architecture Working prototypes in weeks AI-generated test suites CI/CD pipelines Living documentation Clean handover
02

AI-powered product management

Product leadership that uses AI to turn research, feedback and data into clear decisions. We use AI to synthesise discovery, draft specs and user stories, keep the backlog clean and report progress, while a product owner sets priorities and protects the vision.

Ideal for: founders without a product team, and teams whose backlog has grown faster than their roadmap.

What you get

AI-assisted discovery PRDs & user stories Backlog grooming Sprint planning Outcome-based roadmaps Automated status reports
03

AI features for your product

We add LLMs, agents and intelligent automation to the products and workflows you already have. We start with a short feasibility review, build the feature against your real data, and ship it with evaluation, guardrails, monitoring and a fallback for when the model gets it wrong.

Ideal for: in-product assistants, search and summarisation, document processing, and workflow automation.

What you get

Feasibility review LLM & agent integration Retrieval (RAG) pipelines Evaluation suites Guardrails & fallbacks Cost & quality monitoring
04

Maintenance & modernisation

We provide ongoing support for software in production, including systems we didn't build. AI helps us learn an unfamiliar codebase quickly, refactor safely and get through dependency upgrades that have been put off for too long.

Ideal for: legacy systems, inherited codebases, and products that need a dependable team behind them.

What you get

AI-assisted code audits Monitoring & alerting Bug fixes & upgrades Refactoring Agreed response times Monthly health reports
